How the numbers are made: ERA5 daily global anomalies (Copernicus Climate Pulse) are averaged into monthly means; a per-month OLS regression (1990–2025) translates ERA5 into NOAA's settlement scale; incomplete months use a two-predictor conditional forecast (previous month + observed days); the record threshold (+0.005 for 2-decimal rounding) is compared against a Normal(μ,σ) with σ combining forecast and translation residuals. Full derivation: Model_Analysis.ipynb · market analytics: Kalshi.ipynb.
