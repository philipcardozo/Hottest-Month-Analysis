How the numbers are made: ERA5 daily global anomalies (Copernicus Climate Pulse) → monthly means; per-month OLS (1990–2025) translates ERA5 to NOAA's settlement scale; the incomplete month uses a two-predictor conditional forecast (June mean + observed days), and the ECMWF-augmented view extends observed days with the IFS 15-day global-mean forecast (Open-Meteo, anchored to ERA5 overlap days, horizon edge dropped — fetch_forecast.py) with forecast-error inflation. Record threshold +0.005 (2-decimal rounding) vs Normal(μ,σ). Full derivation: Model_Analysis.ipynb · markets: Kalshi.ipynb · robustness: overfit_test.py.

Updates: launchd job com.hottestmonth.daily refreshes at 07:15 and appends track_log.csv; manual: python3 update_data.py. Page reloads data.js every 5 min over HTTP (python3 -m http.server 8000 → http://localhost:8000/Dashboard.html).

Not financial advice. Assumptions documented in the notebooks — normality, mapping stationarity, NWP forecast trust (see honest bracket).
